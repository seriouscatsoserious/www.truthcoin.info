### 2025

* Ran a [weekly summer hackathon](https://www.drivechain.info/blog/perf-contest/) -- ultimately reaching [303s / 10x 960MB blocks] -- enough for Bitcoin to process [all the txns on Earth](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/all-world-txns/).
* L2L releases a 2nd testnet -- this one a Core-clone (full 920k+ blocks, and full 800 GB txn history).
* Accepted an [invitation](https://github.com/TABConf/7.tabconf.com/issues/95) to debate a whole room full of lightning devs at once. (See [the results](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EhJoJPf76Pw) for yourself.)


### 2024

* Produced [CUSF](https://bip300cusf.com/) -- a way of doing any soft fork, without modifying Bitcoin Core at all.
* Produced a working testnet, demoing Bip300 with fake coins -- [try it](https://layertwolabs.com/download).

### 2023

* Gathered 50+ [endorsements from the top Bitcoin minds](https://layertwolabs.com/friends).
* Named #27th [most influential](https://www.coindesk.com/most-influential-2023) person in Crypto. 
* Appeared on [Nic Carter's podcast](https://castleisland.vc/paul-sztorc-drivechain-on-bitcoin-security-budget-and-the-importance-merge-mining-ep-280/) -- Nic referred to me as the "Steely Dan" of Bitcoin -- ie, the Bitcoin expert's expert.


### 2022

* Wrote "Lightning Limitations" (which inspired several copycats) -- first as [mailing list posts](https://lists.linuxfoundation.org/pipermail/bitcoin-dev/2022-February/020018.html) and second as [a blog post](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/lightning-limitations/). Finally the emperor was revealed to have no clothes, and other people were [finally comfortable agreeing](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/ln-blackpill/) -- this led to the collapse of the Bitcoin Orthodox ("lightning for payments") and the [rise of 'Bitcoin Season 2'](https://news.bitcoin.com/brad-mills-reflects-on-bitcoin-season-2-a-new-era-of-monetary-and-platform-coexistence/)
* Produced [EthSide](https://www.drivechain.info/blog/ethside-guide/) -- a Bitcoin L2 which is a perfect clone of an Ethereum geth full node.
* Raised $3 M to create [LayerTwo Labs](https://layertwolabs.com/) -- "Making every transaction a Bitcoin txn".


### 2021

* Published three essays on the future of Bitcoin -- [scaling](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/thunder/), [privacy](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/zside-meltcast/), and [fundamental value](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/bitnames/).
* Published [a sequel](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/security-budget-ii-mm/) to my "Security Budget" article.
* (All of these essays are way ahead of their time.)


### 2020

* Organized & managed a special contest, to create a [zk-snark drivechain](https://www.drivechain.info/blog/spring-contest/).


### 2019

* Wrote [Security Budget in the Long Run](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/security-budget/) -- way ahead of its time.
* Someone on the internet [really liked](https://old.reddit.com/r/slatestarcodex/comments/c429xc/mapterritory_epistemology_a_5_part_series_by_paul/) my ["Map-Territory Epistemology"](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/mt-1/)
* I wrote ["The Consent of the Governed"](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/consent-of-the-governed/) -- a rejection of Spooner's anarchism.


### 2018

* Created & released the [first drivechain](https://www.drivechain.info/blog/first-release/).
* Published [Deniability](https://www.truthcoin.info/blog/deniability/) -- a privacy tool, [beloved](https://x.com/alexkravets/status/1624629204708118528) [by](https://x.com/alexkravets/status/1780843855728030033) [Alex](https://x.com/alexkravets/status/1561227244218945536) Kravets (creator of Google autocomplete), and later a [pull request into Bitcoin Core](https://github.com/bitcoin-core/gui/pull/733) (still not merged) -- and [implmented by me](https://www.drivechain.info/releases/) in DriveNet.


### 2017

* Appeared on [the Developer Table](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/Segwit_support) to reject SegWit2x.
* On the Program Committee for Scaling IV (Stanford).
* Still getting [job offers from Blockstream](https://x.com/Truthcoin/status/1267432543843946496).
* Published two BIPs: [300](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0300.mediawiki) and [301](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0301.mediawiki).


### 2016

* Two mammoth presentations that --to this day-- are ahead of their time:
* * May -- ["Sidechain Privatization"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xGu0o8HH10U&list=PLw8-6ARlyVciMH79ZyLOpImsMug3LgNc4&index=1)
* * Sept -- ["Sidechains: The Case Against Worrying"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0goYH2sDw0w&list=PLw8-6ARlyVciNjgS_NFhAu-qt7HPf_dtg&index=1)
* Oct 8 -- Scaling III [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gzg_u9gHc5Q&t=6575s) -- about *shrinking the blocksize* and getting greater scale.
* Yes, I was always [a smallblocker](https://x.com/Truthcoin/status/1988021934802514362).


### 2015

* Jan 28 -- Vitalik [replies](https://blog.ethereum.org/2015/01/28/p-epsilon-attack) to my "P+e attack" (which I had previously raised, in the [comments section of his "SchellingCoin"](https://blog.ethereum.org/2014/03/28/schellingcoin-a-minimal-trust-universal-data-feed) which seems to no longer exist)
* Aug 4 -- Published ["Nothing is Cheaper Than Proof-of-Work"](http://www.truthcoin.info/blog/pow-cheapest/) which quickly became famous (cited [here](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/Clearing_Up_Misconceptions_About_Full_Nodes) among other places).
* Nov 24 -- Published ["Drivechain"](http://www.truthcoin.info/blog/drivechain/).
* Spoke at both Scaling I (Montreal) and Scaling II (Hong Kong)


### 2014

* May 3 -- Truthcoin ("Bitcoin Prediction Markets") [rose to the top of Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7691289). Apparently [it is viewed favorably](https://slatestarcodex.com/2014/07/30/meditations-on-moloch/) by Scott Alexander of slatestarcodex.
* May 27 -- Created ["Truthcoin Talk"](https://forum.truthcoin.info/index.php/topic,4.msg4.html#msg4) forum. It is quickly joined by vitalik, gwern, and "toast" aka Nikolai Mushegian (who later created MakerDAO / was [possibly assassinated by the CIA](https://thechainsaw.com/technology/cia-nikolai-mushegian-pronounced-dead-in-puerto-rico/))
* June 9 -- I [discuss truthcoin](https://fountain.fm/episode/q33eXLFNrVvvDNBm8a9w) on the OG "Let's Talk Bitcoin" show.
* Novemeber -- I am offered a partnership by Blockstream.
* December -- I am offered a full-time Bitcoin job, by Roger Ver.


### 2013

* Dec 2, 2013 - Published "Truthcoin" -- [decentralized bitcoin prediction markets](https://github.com/psztorc/Truthcoin) -- this concept later became Augur, Gnosis, and led to ChainLink, PolyMarket, etc.


### Pre-Bitcoin

* 2010-2012 - Obtained 2x Masters degrees in 2 years. GMAT (99th percentile).
* 2007-2010 - Obtained 2x BA degrees in 3 years. SAT (98th percentile).
